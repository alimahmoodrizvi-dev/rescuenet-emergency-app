Privacy Policy for RescueNet

Last updated: October 2026

RescueNet ("the app", "we", "our") is an emergency reporting and coordination application. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and who it may be shared with. It applies to the RescueNet mobile app and the RescueNet Command Center dashboard used by coordinators and volunteers.

Information we collect

Data typeWhy we collect it
Location (precise/approximate) To tag emergency reports with a location, show nearby incidents to coordinators, and suggest nearby help to users.
Bluetooth & nearby device signals To relay emergency reports between nearby devices over a local mesh network when internet access is unavailable. This is used only for message relay, never for tracking or advertising.
Audio (microphone) Used only when you choose to submit a voice-based incident report; the recording is processed to extract report details.
Photos (camera) Used only if you choose to attach a photo to an incident report.
Account information Name and contact details you provide when reporting an incident, marking yourself safe, or registering as a volunteer/coordinator.
Incident report content The description, type, and severity of an incident you submit.

How we use this information

Data collected by RescueNet is used solely to route emergency reports to coordinators and rescue resources, to notify your chosen contacts of your safety status, and to help coordinators allocate help during an emergency. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share data with

Incident and location data submitted through the app is shared with RescueNet coordinators and volunteers who use the Command Center dashboard, so they can respond to reported emergencies. We do not share your data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Data may be disclosed if required by law.

Data storage and security

Report data is stored on RescueNet's backend servers. While offline, data may be temporarily stored on your device and relayed to nearby devices over Bluetooth before reaching the server. We take reasonable technical measures to protect this data, but no system is perfectly secure, and sharing information during an emergency always carries some inherent risk.

Your choices

Location, Bluetooth, camera, and microphone permissions can be turned off at any time in your device settings, though doing so may limit core features of the app (such as reporting your location or using offline relay). You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time using the contact details below.

Children's privacy

RescueNet is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
